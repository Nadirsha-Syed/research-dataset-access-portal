About the Repository

Dedicated to sharing open and controlled research data to support scientific collaboration.

Our Mission

The SR University Research Dataset Repository (SRU-RDR) serves as a central hub for researchers and students globally to query datasets generated by SRU laboratories, affiliates, and academic partners. We provide secure infrastructure to host large-scale scientific datasets across biomedicine, atmospheric science, social sciences, and engineering.

Data Governance & Safety

Sharing academic data requires careful consideration of participant privacy, commercial confidentiality, and intellectual property. The repository categorizes datasets into three security levels:

  • Open Access: Freely downloadable datasets representing anonymized aggregates, simulated trials, or public domain facts.
  • Restricted Access: Datasets requiring researcher validation, institutional email authentication, and standard citations agreements.
  • Controlled Access: Highly sensitive datasets containing detailed clinical records, genetics, or location logs. Access requires a detailed IRB approval, a signed Data Use Agreement (DUA), and secure computing environments.

Citation Guidelines

When utilizing datasets hosted at SRU, publications must acknowledge the researchers who generated the dataset and include the unique digital object identifier (DOI) associated with it. A standard format is available on each dataset's detail page.

Policy Oversight

All dataset requests are audited by the SRU Institutional Data Review Board (IDRB) to ensure compliance with human subject protection, HIPAA regulations, and collaborative data covenants.

For questions regarding compliance or to host your own dataset on our platform, please read our hosting documentation or reach out directly to the compliance department.
